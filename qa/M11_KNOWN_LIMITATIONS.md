Anonymous identity also depends on retained browser authentication storage; there is no implemented cross-device account recovery.
5. Gemini remains an optional, explicit, fictional-data-only mode in the existing assistant and extraction interface. Rules and eligibility requirements remain deterministic. Model explanations and heuristic safety filters do not prove exhaustive hallucination or prompt-injection resistance.

7. Provider quota/permission/model failures, timeout, malformed outputs and missing ADC are primarily verified through controlled mocks. No large billable load test, forced production Firestore outage, or exhaustive live network-fault campaign was performed.

Its success path is covered by the full automated regression. OCR was not called again solely to repeat the same billable success path.
9. Vertex quota metrics were readable, but the saved quota query was paginated. Successful minimal inference demonstrates access at test time, not reserved capacity or immunity to future 429 responses.
